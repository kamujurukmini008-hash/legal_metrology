Check Packaged Commodity Labels in Seconds

Scan a product label and see whether it carries the declarations required by the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

Start Scanning View Dashboard

Why this matters

Every packed product must show the details the law asks for - who made it, how much is inside, the real price (MRP) and more. Missing or wrong details can cheat buyers. Checking labels by hand is slow, so this tool does a quick first check and shows the evidence for every result.

How it works

1. Upload

Upload or capture a photo of the product label.

2. Read

The image is cleaned and OCR reads the label text.

3. Check

A rule engine checks each mandatory declaration.

4. Report

Get a status for every field, with evidence, and print it as a PDF.

Commodity name
Manufacturer / packer / importer
Net quantity

Month and year of manufacture
Consumer care details

Expiry date (food, medicine, cosmetics)

Healthy ingredient check

For food products, we also read the ingredients, warn about items like trans fat, artificial colours and too much sugar, and suggest healthier swaps with search links for Amazon, Blinkit and Zepto.

Result statuses

Detected · Missing · Review Required · Potential Non-Compliance

This tool is a screening aid, not a substitute for authorised legal inspection.

Limitations

Reading text from photos (OCR) can make mistakes, especially on shiny, colourful or blurry labels, so you verify the details before the final check. Rules are based on the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 and should be confirmed against the official text. Ingredient advice is general awareness, not medical advice. Swap links open search pages and do not show live prices.
